Algebra 1 · California standards

Standards diagnostic

Use this short check to identify strengths and decide what to practice next.

Before you begin

Answer 18 multiple-choice questions across six broad standards areas. Allow about 30–40 minutes. Use scratch paper and a calculator if helpful. Questions are adapted from linked Illustrative Mathematics student practice sets.

This is a brief diagnostic, not a full assessment of every California standard. Three questions per area provide limited evidence; each standard code identifies only the skill sampled. You can leave questions blank and see them as unanswered.

Assessment report

Results show performance on the sampled questions, not full mastery of a standard. Unanswered items count as incorrect in the score and are shown separately. Each area contains only three questions.

Performance by standards area

3 correct: Strong on this sample · 2: Developing · 0–1: Practice needed · No answers: Not assessed. These are informal indicators, not official proficiency levels.

Question review

Review explanations and use the linked practice sets to continue learning.

Coverage

The report covers only the listed skills. Other crosswalk areas and standards—including sequences, inverse functions, graphing, transformations, and many modeling and statistics skills—are not comprehensively assessed. Consult the Algebra I section of the crosswalk for the full list and lesson connections.
